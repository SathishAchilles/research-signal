## Template: meeting preparation

1. **Backing**: as the use-case backing template (evidence by rung, strongest results, gaps).
2. **Direction**: as the field direction template, shortened to the Situation / Complication / Implication paragraphs and 3 signals to watch.
3. **Flagged for this client**: the papers from `my_log` tagged for the client, each with its citation and the consultant's note. If none are tagged, say so.
4. **Likely questions**: 3 to 5 questions the client may ask, each with a short cited answer.
